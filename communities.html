Mankessim Central

The heartbeat of trade and tradition. Central Mankessim serves as the traditional seat of power and the primary commercial nerve center for the Mfantsiman community, housing the ancient Posuban shrines.

Chief: Nana Ama Amissah III

Transit Hub

Anomabo Junction

A critical gateway connecting the coastal historical sites to the inland markets. This community has evolved from a simple rest stop into a thriving residential and educational enclave.

Chief: Kantamanto Kweku III

18k Pop.
5 Schools
1 Health
Ancestral Lands

Nkusukum

Home to the Nkusukum Traditional Area, this community preserves the foundational myths and the bravery of the Bore Bore Fante settlers who founded Mankessim.

Chief: Nana Okese Essandoh IX

22k Pop.
